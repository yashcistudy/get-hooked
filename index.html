case · dastan honey

konar.

a perfume ad,
except it's honey.

 

no jar. no label.
still on time.

Dastan needed its launch film before Dastan had a product to film. The labels weren't printed and the jars weren't ready, but the launch date was.

So the brief came with a list of wants: real people, real honey, the stuff that didn't exist yet, and a hard date. We shot what was real and made what wasn't.

  1. humanMoj on camera, Jaz behind it and in front of it. Real timing, real chase, a real scream.
  2. naturalWild sidr honey from our family's hives in the Zagros mountains, poured and shot for real.
  3. aiThe perfume bottle, the honey mist and the labelled jar. The only labelled jar in the film never existed.
  4. motionSupers, the swap, and one grain over every shot, so live and AI read as one camera.
  5. on timeDelivered before launch, in 16:9 for the brand and 9:16 for the feed.
